Research candidates are promoted explicitly. Production inference lives in
`src/zebrafish/ratings`, and released artifact checksums live in
`resources/models/registry.json`. Training data, installed runtimes and generated
runs remain local. Historical reports describe the experiments at their original
dates; they do not automatically change the application's current model.
